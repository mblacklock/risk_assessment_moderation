# Laboratory and workspace nomenclature

Working glossary, based on staff clarification during v2 evaluation on
18 November 2026 (Europe/London).

This records local naming and interpretation. It is documentation for a later
checking update; it does not change the frozen v2 rules, prompts, findings or
approvals. Room suitability and permission to undertake work remain staff and
supervisor responsibilities.

## Confirmed naming structure

| Component or name | Staff-confirmed meaning | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EL` | Ellison Building | Building prefix; not a specific room on its own. |
| `C` after `EL` | Block C | `ELC` identifies Ellison Building, Block C. A block reference alone does not identify a room. |
| `E` after `EL` | Block E | Apply the same building/block naming structure. Staff confirmed that Ellison C- and E-block rooms are labs for this broad space-type comparison. |
| Room number following the building/block reference | Identifies the room | Read the building, block and room together. Preserve room-number digits, leading zeroes and any supplied suffix; do not guess omitted characters. |
| `SAN` spaces | IT only | Recognise SAN-coded rooms as IT spaces. Do not infer a physical manufacturing/testing laboratory from a SAN room code. |
| The Zone | IT hub | Recognise this as an IT workspace, not evidence of a physical printing or mechanical-testing space. |
| Metal Fabrication Work Shop | A recognised, acceptable workshop name for fabrication | Staff confirmed that tests cannot be carried out there. Where testing is planned, its workspace must also be identified in Area/Activity. |
| `EBC` / `EBE` prefixes | Nonstandard forms, not official equivalents of the Ellison prefixes | Staff would probably accept these during moderation when the intended block/room is clear. Retain the naming defect separately from any decision to accept it. The proposed student self-checker should flag the prefix for correction. |

The code structure describes room identity and the staff-confirmed broad space
type, not equipment, risk adequacy or authorisation for an exact procedure. This
check does not inspect individual rooms' equipment, capabilities or availability.
Those matters remain with the supervisor and technical staff.

## Boundary between the two checks

Staff clarified this distinction during the v2 adjudication:

| Check | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Specific area or activity | The Area/Activity entry clearly identifies the workspace or activity. |
| Activities-facilities consistency | The declared spaces cover and are compatible with all planned work. |

Assess compatibility at the broad space-type level: laboratory, fabrication
workshop or IT space. Staff confirmed that Ellison C/E rooms count as labs here;
do not require a room-by-room catalogue or confirmation that a specific room has
a particular instrument. SAN and The Zone count as IT spaces. Retain explicit
staff-confirmed restrictions, such as tests not being permitted in Metal
Fabrication Work Shop, without expanding this into an equipment-suitability audit.

For example, Metal Fabrication Work Shop is identifiable, so its name passes the
first check. If the project also includes tests that staff have confirmed cannot
be carried out there, the consistency check requires Issue found. Do not fail the
location-name check merely to represent that separate incompatibility.

A vague entry such as Engineering or Risk Assessment still fails the first check.
Where no physical workspace can be established, a reliable facilities comparison
may have to wait for clarification; do not invent an incompatibility. Review
earlier audit category selections case by case rather than automatically treating
every originally recorded Complete as a reliable reference judgement. Preserve
original audit answers and superseded adjudications alongside the revised decision.

## Applying the nomenclature to Area/Activity

The standard template has an **Area/Activity** field. A technician should be able
to identify the assessed work/workspace directly from that entry without reading
the whole document to reconstruct its scope. Do not require a new Location field.

- A building/block-only entry such as `ELC` needs clarification for physical work
  unless the Area/Activity entry itself otherwise identifies the assessed scope
  sufficiently. Do not borrow missing specificity from risk rows or ethics text.
- All planned work must be covered by the declared spaces. Assess that coverage
  under Activities-facilities consistency: IT spaces do not cover fabrication or
  mechanical testing, and a fabrication workshop does not cover tests that staff
  have confirmed cannot be undertaken there.
- An established local functional name, such as Metal Fabrication Work Shop, may
  identify a space clearly without a room code. Do not impose a code-only format.
- Continue the agreed interpretation for computer-work sections: a clear
  computer-based activity or identifiable computer workspace can be sufficient.
- Actual RA and ethics activities provide context for checking whether the
  declared scope covers the planned work. They do not repair a generic or
  incomplete Area/Activity entry.

## Equivalent names and uncertainty

This glossary supplies local context; it is not an exhaustive list of permitted
answer strings. Recognise ordinary differences in capitalisation, spacing,
punctuation and equivalent wording by meaning. Do not invent room identities or
equipment from a plausible-looking code.

For example, the AI may recognise **Mechanical Engineering test lab** as a
description of a physical testing space. Whether it identifies one particular
local facility clearly enough is not yet confirmed. Do not map it automatically
to a specific room or assume every testing procedure is permitted there.

Use moderator review when an alias, incomplete code or space description is
ambiguous. Clear missing scope or an established incompatible workspace requires
correction. Unknown terminology alone does not establish that a student is wrong.

### Nonstandard Ellison prefixes

Staff confirmed that `EBC` and `EBE` are not accepted official prefixes. Do not
silently add them as canonical aliases or mark their spelling as technically
correct. In moderator use, staff may accept a clearly interpretable intended
Ellison C/E room without requiring resubmission. That operational decision does
not remove the original naming defect or establish that the space suits the work.

For the proposed student self-checker, flag those nonstandard prefixes and ask the
student to verify and use the official building/block/room designation. Preserve
the student's block and room information; do not guess missing digits, choose
between conflicting codes or invent a particular room. This is a recorded future
requirement; no student self-checker is implemented by this document.
